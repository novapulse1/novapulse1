Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding HRMS integration, biometric devices, and our growth services.

Can your HRMS integrate with physical biometric attendance machines?

Yes. Our HRMS workflows connect directly with fingerprint, face recognition, and RFID biometric machines via LAN, Wi-Fi, or cloud push data, syncing employee punch logs in real time.

How does automated payroll processing work?

The system automatically calculates work days, late marks, leaves, and overtime from biometric logs, applies statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS), and generates 1-click payslips.

Can we manage multi-location branch attendance from one centralized portal?

Yes. All branch biometric machines push records to a central cloud server, giving head office management a unified attendance dashboard across Delhi NCR, UP, and national branches.

What industries do your hiring and lead generation services cover?

We specialize in B2B corporate sectors, healthcare/surgicals, SaaS, tech staffing, manufacturing, and distribution businesses requiring vetted talent and qualified sales pipelines.

Do you provide on-site hardware setup and implementation support?

Yes. Our team assists with physical machine installation, network routing, software payroll mapping, and administrative staff training.
